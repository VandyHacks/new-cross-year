# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students exploring Vanderbilt's student-run hackathon, returning participants,
and prospective sponsors, judges, and mentors.

## Product Purpose

Introduce VandyHacks XIII, demonstrate the organization's history, and help
visitors register interest or get involved.

## Brand Personality

Curious, creative, welcoming. The current website uses a vinyl-record motif,
navy and purple colors, and music-inspired movement. The user confirmed that
the impact timeline should retain this direction.

## Anti-references

Generic dashboards, unsupported impact claims, and lengthy scroll sequences
that prevent visitors from seeing the history at a glance.

## Design Principles

- Make past editions explorable without taking over page scrolling.
- Derive impact totals from the same records used by the timeline.
- Preserve the distinction between registrations and attendance, and between
  listed prize value and cash awarded.
- Keep the existing typography, colors, and record navigation consistent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, touch scrolling, readable contrast,
and reduced motion. Keep event details available as semantic HTML.
